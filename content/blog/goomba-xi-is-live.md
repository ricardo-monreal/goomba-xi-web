Welcome to **Goomba XI**, a game preservation server for Level 75 era FINAL FANTASY XI.

We play the game as it was before *Wings of the Goddess*: the original nations, Rise of the Zilart, Chains of Promathia and Treasures of Aht Urhgan, with the level cap raised through Maat's trials. Trusts are on, so you can adventure solo or with a few friends. Era-accurate client data ships with the launcher, so menus, shops and dialogue match the time too.

## Getting in

1. [Create your account](/register) on this website.
2. Download the launcher for [Windows or Linux](/getting-started).
3. Sign in to the launcher with the same username and password, press **Play**, and make your character.

## Where to find us

News is posted here on the blog. Check the [rules](/rules) and the [approved addons](/approved-addons) before you set out.

See you in Vana'diel.
